GEMS
Games as a
Multi-Layered
Security Thread
Protecting citizens from extremist recruitment in the online gaming ecosystem.
Key Outcomes

  • Enhance the scientific knowledge base and develop a novel academic discipline — Sociology of Gaming and Radicalization.

  • Understand methods of preselection and extremist grooming of vulnerable individuals in the gaming space.

  • Provide EPAs with training materials, methods and tools to effectively tackle extremism in gaming.

Broad Scope

Computer, mobile, console games and streaming platforms — the full modern gaming ecosystem.

By minimising recruitment at its roots, GEMS protects EU citizens where they are most vulnerable.

Who Are We?

GEMS is a Horizon-funded project to stop extremist recruitment in online gaming environments.

The Problem: Extremist groups exploit in-game communication to identify, groom, and recruit vulnerable players — particularly young people.

The Watchtower Platform

Detects and disrupts grooming attempts automatically — catching radicalization before it takes hold.

The Radicalization Pipeline

How extremists exploit the gaming space:

  • 1
    Online Multiplayer EntryPlayer enters an environment with embedded communication tools.
  • 2
    PreselectionExtremists identify vulnerable individuals using behavioural cues.
  • 3
    Soft RadicalizationGradual exposure to extremist ideas through casual in-game chat.
  • 4
    Hard RecruitmentFull recruitment into extremist networks, insulated by established trust.
